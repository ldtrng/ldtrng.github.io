Cross Stitch Pattern Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Cross Stitch Pattern Tracker (“the app”, package io.ld2t.cross_stitch) turns cross stitch charts into a workspace for marking stitches and tracking progress. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

Data the app accesses

  • Chart files you import (PDF and images) – copied into the app’s private storage on your device so you can view and mark them. The original files are not changed.
  • Your projects – grid settings, threads, stitch marks, stitching sessions and progress photos are stored in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Photos you add – progress photos are taken or picked only when you choose to, and are stored in the app’s private storage.
  • Images you save – when you save a finished preview, the image is written to your device’s Pictures folder at your request.
  • Backups – backup files are written only where you choose: a file you share or save, or a folder you pick for automatic backup. If you pick a folder that is synced by another service, that service’s own policy applies to the copy.

All chart reading and symbol recognition runs on your device. None of this data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned on your Google account and stores that result on your device. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Internet access

The app uses the network only to talk to Google Play for the purchase and to check whether the purchase is owned. It does not upload your charts, projects or photos.

Data sharing

The app shares no data with third parties.

Data deletion

You can delete any project inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local data. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
